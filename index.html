01
Master’s thesis Apr 2026 — present

Combining probability forecasts,
without training another model.

I develop train-free ensembles that select or dynamically weight calibrated forecasts, and use proper scoring rule decompositions to study their Brier risk.

Supervised by Hyukjun Gweon, Hao Yu, and Reginald Kulperger at Western University.

02
Collaborative research Ongoing

Reliable predictions from
incomplete clinical records.

I study class-conditional conformal prediction for Parkinsonian classification using calibrated ensembles, examining how missing information affects coverage and prediction-set size.

Analysis of 3,434 Canadian Open Parkinson Network participants, with nested validation and participant-cluster bootstrap intervals.
