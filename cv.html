Uncertainty and Calibration in Train-Free Probabilistic Ensembles

Master’s thesis · Western University · April 2026 – present
Supervisors: Hyukjun Gweon, Hao Yu, and Reginald Kulperger

  • Develop train-free ensembles that select or dynamically weight calibrated forecasts by distance from the class prior.
  • Analyse Brier risk using proper scoring rule decomposition to study forecast spread and calibration alignment.
  • Built logit-additive simulations with calibrated marginal forecasts; P-extreme lowered mean expected Brier score from 0.1753 (equal averaging) to 0.1585 and outperformed the better base in 87.8% of matrices.

Conformal Prediction for Parkinsonian Classification

Ongoing

  • Modelled three-class outcomes for 3,434 Canadian Open Parkinson Network participants using an ensemble of CatBoost and two-stage hierarchical classifiers with Dirichlet calibration.
  • Evaluated class-conditional conformal sets across 21 model and missingness scenarios with three repeats of five-fold outer validation and participant-cluster bootstrap intervals.
  • Quantified coverage-efficiency trade-offs: a narrower policy reduced overall mean set size by 11.1% versus the robust union, but healthy-control coverage was 81.0% in the highest information-deficit stratum (90% target).
